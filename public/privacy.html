Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: we don't track you. This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party trackers. We don't collect personal information unless you choose to send it to us through our contact form.

Information we don't collect

Browsing this site does not require you to identify yourself. We do not set cookies, build profiles, use analytics services, or sell, rent or share personal information with anyone.

Information you choose to send us

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you enter. That information is delivered to us by email so we can reply to you. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and any follow-up conversation you start. We don't store form submissions in a database, and we don't add you to any mailing list.

Service providers

To run this site we rely on a small number of infrastructure providers, each of which processes data only as needed to provide its service:

  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers this site. Like any web host, its servers process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile protects the contact form from spam. It may process limited technical signals from your browser to tell humans from bots. It does not use cookies for advertising.
  • Our email provider delivers contact-form messages to our inbox.

Links to other sites

This site links to projects we've worked on. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Children

This site is not directed to children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

If you've contacted us and would like us to delete your correspondence, just ask through the contact form and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
